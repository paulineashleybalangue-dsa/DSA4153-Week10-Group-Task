## Final Verification

Before submission, verify that:

- Each member's contribution has been committed.
- All pull requests have been reviewed and approved.
- Approved contributions are merged into the final branch.
- The final project has been independently cloned, executed, and tested.
- Independent verification evidence is documented in `INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION.md`.
